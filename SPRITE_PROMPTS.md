# Sprite Generation Prompts — one per character

Paste these **one at a time** into your image generator. Each is self-contained (style + grid + character). Files go to `/public/sprites/`.

**Reality check:** most generators won't nail a clean 6×4 / 48 px grid first try. If it struggles, ask it for **one big single pose** (or **one 288×48 direction strip at a time**) and assemble the 288×192 sheet yourself. Whatever you do, the final file must be **exactly 288×192, transparent**.

---

## 1 — `elder.png` (Maeve the Elder)

```
Dark fantasy NPC sprite sheet, character only, TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND.

STYLE: medieval dark fantasy, painterly pixel art, muted desaturated palette (deep charcoals, oxblood red, tarnished gold, cold candlelight), grim atmospheric mood, subtle magical glow accents. Detailed but readable at small size. No bright or cheerful colours.

LAYOUT: one 288x192 px sheet = 6 columns x 4 rows of 48x48 px cells. Character ~32 px tall, horizontally centred, feet on the bottom baseline, identical position and scale in every cell (no drift, no zoom changes).
Rows top to bottom = facing: Down (toward camera), Left, Right (mirror of Left is fine), Up (back turned).
Columns left to right = frames: Idle, Walk contact (left foot fwd), Walk passing, Walk contact (right foot fwd), Walk passing (alt), Work pose (item raised).

CHARACTER: Maeve the Elder — an ancient hooded ARCHMAGE, tattered star-flecked robes, gnarled hands, faintly glowing eyes. ITEM: a tall staff with a cold blue glowing tip.

REMINDERS: transparent background; keep feet on a fixed baseline across all frames; final output exactly 288x192 px.
```
